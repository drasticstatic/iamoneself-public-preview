# Privacy Policy

### I Am One Self — The Holy Earth Foundation

This portal is a **pure static website** — no server, no database, no
account system, and no server-side runtime at deploy time. It collects and
stores no personal data on its own. This page spells out exactly what
happens to information you share here, and where it actually goes.

See [`how-it-works`](how-it-works.md) for the technical mechanics behind
the intake form and the guide agents, and the site's own persistent
disclaimer banner for the compliance framing this policy sits alongside.

---

## What this site does and doesn't collect

- No third-party advertising or behavioral-tracking scripts run on this
  site.
- No cookies, accounts, or analytics identifiers are set by this portal
  itself.
- The static pages you browse — FAQ, Retreats, Teachings, Plants &
  Miracles — involve no data collection at all.

## What happens when you submit the intake form

- A completed application is relayed to the foundation team — either
  through a third-party form processor ([Formspree](https://formspree.io),
  which keeps a submission record the team can review, export, and delete)
  or through your own email client when you press send. **The prototype
  itself never transmits or retains it.**
- Application information is shared only with the team and the medical
  advisor, for the purpose of reviewing your retreat application and
  supporting your integration. It is never sold, and never shared with
  advertisers or unrelated third parties.
- By submitting an application you consent to the team contacting you
  about your application and integration support; you may ask to be
  removed from that contact at any time.
- To request a copy of, or the deletion of, any submission you've sent,
  reach the team through the [contact page](https://www.iamoneself.com/about-the-speaker)
  or any of the listing partners below, and the record will be exported or
  removed on request.

## Where a booking or payment actually happens

This portal doesn't process bookings or payments itself. Every retreat
registration and payment completes on a verified third-party platform:

- [Retreat Guru](https://iamoneself.retreat.guru)
- [Aya Advisors](https://www.ayaadvisors.org)
- [The Third Wave](https://www.thethirdwave.co)
- the official [iamoneself.com](https://www.iamoneself.com) Wix site

Each of those platforms has its own privacy policy governing the
information you share with them directly — this policy covers only what
happens on this dev portal.

## Questions

Reach the foundation team through the
[contact page](https://www.iamoneself.com/about-the-speaker) with any
privacy question, correction request, or deletion request.
